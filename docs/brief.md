# knots

> The Productive Social App

An app so you can finally lock in yourself and with your friends, make commitments real, not just another promise you forget and feel guilty about. Knots turn the things you want to do into commitments you can actually follow through on. Tie yourself to a goal, bring a friend along, and make progress together.

## Problem statement

The idea started with a simple observation: we spend hours doomscrolling and mostly just send TikToks to our friends. Filipinos have the second-highest time online in the world, and about half of Gen Z in the US say they feel lonely on weekends. Knots swaps the video for a promise, so staying in touch means you end up doing something together.

Sending TikToks back and forth is really a low-effort way of saying "I'm thinking about you."

## Features

- **Tie a knot:** Pick a goal, hobby, or plan and tie yourself to it, alone or with friends.
- **A rope that shows the truth:** It tightens when you show up and loosens when you drift, so you can see how it's going without any numbers.
    - **One-tap check-ins:** Tap, snap a photo, or leave a quick note. It takes about as long as forwarding a TikTok.
- **Tug:** Send a friend a little nudge when a knot is getting slack.
- **Gentle nudges:** A loose knot gets a friendly "want to tighten this together?" and no guilt trip.
- **Rest mode:** Pause a knot when life gets busy, with no penalty to you or your friends. pausing a knot or letting it "rest" without it counting against anyone
- **Small circles:** Built for 2 to 5 close friends, so the knot always has someone on the other end.
- **Knot types:** Pick how you commit.
    - **Tied knot (1-on-1):** You and a friend stay accountable together with shared progress and deadlines.
    - **Squad knot (2-5):** Small group where everyone checks in daily to keep the group knot tight.
    - **Anchor knot (solo):** Personal track tied to a community milestone, public or private.
    - **Knot variations:** Types evolve with connection depth, consistency, and time.
- offline support, knot offline with persistent data, but no access to friends and <AI mascot>
- RAG & Embeddings: memory recall & knots wrapped feature like, offline searching through all your data (knots, friends, messages, etc. all local & offline).
- <AI mascot> uses data too (RAG & embeddings) to nudge, notify, suggest, check up, and help you with your knots.
- Knot trust levels: decrease by not completing, not doing activites and making false promises (just like when someone cheats and breaks ur heart, bad reputation kasi para alam ng others na that certain user is probably has a high chance of not committing, works like how K/D calcuation works in games).
- View stats and info
